Ufone Quiz Tips: Answer Faster, Win More

By Ufone Quiz Today · Updated for 2026 · Helpful guide for Ufone users in Pakistan

Winning the Ufone daily quiz is partly about knowledge and partly about technique. Players who read questions carefully, manage the timer well and practise regularly score higher than players who rely on luck alone. This guide collects practical tips that any player can use from the next round onwards. None of these tips require special tools or tricks, only a little preparation and a calm approach.

Read the whole question first

The single most useful tip is to read the entire question before looking at the answers. Many quiz questions contain a key detail, such as a year, a city or a person's name, that decides the answer. Players who glance at the options first often pick an answer that fits a different question from the one asked. Slow down for one extra second and read every word.

Pay special attention to words like not, except, first, largest and smallest, because they reverse the meaning of a question. A question asking which country is not in Asia has a completely different answer from one asking which country is in Asia, and the difference is a single word.

Use the timer wisely

Most quiz rounds give you a limited time for each question, often around 30 seconds. That is enough time to read and think, but not enough to search for the answer. If you know the answer immediately, tap it and move on, because extra thinking rarely improves a confident answer. If you are unsure, use the time to eliminate the options that are clearly wrong, then choose the best of the rest.

Practise with a timer so the countdown feels familiar. Our Quiz Timer tool on this site gives you 30 seconds per question with score tracking, exactly like a real quiz round. After a few practice rounds, the timer stops feeling stressful and becomes a normal part of the game.

Build general knowledge a little each day

The best long-term way to improve is to learn a little every day. Read the news headlines for ten minutes, especially stories about Pakistan, world events, sports and science. Learn the capitals of major countries, the national symbols of Pakistan, famous scientists and their discoveries, and the winners of major sports events. These topics appear in quizzes again and again.

A general-knowledge book, a free quiz app, or our practice tool can help. Even fifteen minutes of practice a day builds up over a month. Keep a small notebook of questions you got wrong and read about those topics later, because the same topics often return in later rounds.

Play at the right time and place

Play the quiz when you are calm and your internet connection is stable. A weak signal can freeze the quiz or submit an answer you did not choose, and playing while distracted leads to careless mistakes. Find a quiet moment, sit down, and give the round your full attention for a few minutes. Many players play in the morning, when they are fresh and the house is quiet.

Make sure your phone is charged and that you are not about to receive a call that interrupts the quiz. Close other apps that might slow your phone down. These small preparations remove the avoidable reasons for a low score.

Learn from every round

After each round, think about the questions you got wrong. What was the topic? What would the correct answer have been? A quick search after the round, when there is no timer, turns a mistake into knowledge you will keep. Over weeks, the list of topics you have learned grows, and your score rises with it.

Do not be discouraged by a bad round. Even good players have off days, and some questions are simply hard. The quiz is a daily game, so tomorrow brings a fresh set of questions and a fresh chance. The players who improve most are the ones who keep playing and keep learning, not the ones who win once by luck.

Frequently Asked Questions

How can I answer quiz questions faster?
Practise regularly with a timer, learn common general-knowledge topics, and read each question fully before choosing. Speed comes from familiarity, not rushing.

Should I guess if I do not know the answer?
Yes, an educated guess is better than leaving a question blank, since most quiz formats give no points for a skipped question. First eliminate the options that are clearly wrong.

Does practising really help my score?
Yes. Regular practice improves both speed and accuracy, because quiz topics repeat and your reading of questions becomes sharper.
