Ufone Super Card: What's Included and How to Subscribe

By Ufone Quiz Today · Updated for 2026 · Helpful guide for Ufone users in Pakistan

The Ufone Super Card is one of the most popular Ufone bundles because it combines mobile data, call minutes and SMS in a single monthly package. Instead of buying data, minutes and SMS separately, a Super Card gives you a little of everything for one price. This guide explains what is typically included, the different Super Card variants, and how to subscribe. Prices and inclusions change, so always confirm the current details in the official My Ufone app before subscribing.

What a Super Card usually includes

A standard Super Card typically includes a monthly data allowance, a set of on-net minutes for calls to Ufone and PTCL numbers, a smaller set of off-net minutes for other networks, and a bundle of SMS. The exact amounts differ between variants, with larger variants offering more of everything. The bundle is designed for everyday users who call, text and browse in moderate amounts, and who prefer one simple monthly payment instead of managing several small bundles.

Because the amounts change with new offers, this guide does not list fixed figures. The My Ufone app shows the current inclusions and price for each variant before you subscribe, and that screen is the only reliable source. Take a screenshot of the offer before confirming, so you can check later that you received what was promised.

Super Card variants

Ufone has offered several Super Card variants over time, usually in a small, medium and large size. The standard version suits light to moderate users, a Plus version adds more data and minutes for heavier users, and a Max version is aimed at users who need the most of everything. The names and sizes of these variants change, and a variant that existed last year may be replaced by a new one, so check the app for the variants available today.

To choose between them, look at your last month of usage in the app. If you regularly run out of data before the month ends, the next size up is probably worth it. If you finish the month with data and minutes left over, the smaller variant may be enough, and you could save money by switching down. The app's usage history makes this decision easy.

How to subscribe

The simplest way to subscribe is through the My Ufone app. Open the app, go to the bundles or packages section, find the Super Card you want, and tap subscribe. The app shows the price and inclusions before you confirm, and the cost is taken from your balance, so make sure you have enough balance first. You should receive a confirmation message once the bundle is active, and the app's home screen will show your new data and minutes.

You can also subscribe using a USSD code, commonly listed as *770#, though the exact code can change, so confirm it in the app first. Dial the code, follow the on-screen menu, and choose the Super Card variant you want. Keep the confirmation message, because it shows the expiry date of your bundle.

Checking what is left and when it expires

After subscribing, check your remaining data, minutes and SMS in the My Ufone app. The app shows how much of each is left and the date it expires. Super Card bundles are usually monthly, so anything unused at the end of the month normally expires and does not carry over. Knowing your expiry date helps you use the bundle before it runs out, instead of losing it.

If your data runs out early, you can buy a small top-up data bundle, or wait for the next month. If your minutes run out, calls are charged from your main balance at standard rates, so keep an eye on your remaining minutes if you make many calls. The app's usage view is the best place to track all of this.

Is a Super Card worth it for you

A Super Card is worth it if you use data, calls and SMS every month and would otherwise buy them separately. Buying them together is usually cheaper than buying each one on its own, and it is simpler to manage. It is less useful if you only use one of the three, for example if you only need data and never call anyone, because then a data-only bundle may be better value.

Compare the Super Card price with the cost of the separate bundles you would buy anyway. If the Super Card costs less and covers your usage, it is a good choice. If it costs more than your normal spending, stick with separate bundles. Your usage history in the app gives you the numbers you need to make this comparison honestly.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is included in a Ufone Super Card?
Typically a monthly data allowance, on-net and off-net minutes, and SMS. Exact amounts vary by variant, so confirm the current inclusions in the My Ufone app.

How do I subscribe to a Super Card?
Through the My Ufone app's bundles section, or with the USSD code shown in the app. Make sure you have enough balance first.

Does unused Super Card data carry over?
Normally no. Unused data, minutes and SMS usually expire at the end of the monthly validity, so check your expiry date in the app.
