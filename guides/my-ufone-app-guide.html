My Ufone App: Download and Use It for the Quiz

By Ufone Quiz Today · Updated for 2026 · Helpful guide for Ufone users in Pakistan

The My Ufone app is the official self-service app for Ufone customers, and it is also where the daily quiz lives. Through the app you can check your balance, buy packages, view your usage and play the quiz, all without visiting a shop or calling the helpline. This guide walks through downloading the app, signing in, and finding the sections that matter most, including the quiz.

Downloading and installing the app

Android users can download the My Ufone app from the Google Play Store, and iPhone users can get it from the Apple App Store. Search for the official app by name and check that the developer is Ufone before installing, because copycat apps sometimes appear in search results. The app is free to download. Make sure your phone has enough free space and a stable connection before you start the download, so the installation does not fail halfway.

After installation, open the app and allow the basic permissions it asks for, such as notifications if you want quiz reminders. You do not need to grant permissions that seem unrelated to the app's purpose. If the app asks to update itself, let it update, because newer versions include bug fixes and new features such as the latest quiz layout.

Signing in with your Ufone number

To sign in, enter your Ufone mobile number. The app will send a one-time code by SMS to that number. Enter the code in the app to confirm that the number belongs to you. Never share this code with anyone, including people who claim to be from Ufone, because it is the key to your account. If the code does not arrive within a few minutes, check your signal and request a new code.

Once signed in, the app shows your balance, remaining data and active packages on the home screen. Take a moment to look around the home screen, because the quiz card, package offers and rewards are usually shown there. Your account stays signed in, so you normally only need to enter the code again if you sign out or change phones.

Finding the daily quiz

From the home screen, look for a quiz banner, a games section or a rewards tab. The exact position changes when the app is updated, so if you cannot see it, scroll down the home screen or open the menu and look for words like quiz, games or rewards. Tapping the quiz card opens the day's questions. If the card is missing, update the app and check again the next day, because the quiz may be paused between campaigns.

It is a good idea to open the app at the same time each day, so playing the quiz becomes a habit. Many players open it in the morning with their tea, play the round, and then check their balance and packages at the same time. That routine takes only a few minutes.

Checking balance and packages in the app

Your remaining balance is shown at the top of the home screen, and tapping it usually opens a detailed view with your data, minutes and SMS left. This is more reliable than guessing, because the app shows the exact remaining amounts and their expiry dates. You can also see which packages are active on your number and when they expire, which helps you decide whether to buy a new package or wait.

To buy a package, open the bundles or packages section, choose the one you want, and confirm. The app shows the price and what is included before you confirm, so read it carefully. Always confirm the current price in the app itself, because package prices and inclusions change over time and older articles or screenshots may be out of date.

Keeping the app working well

Keep the app updated, clear its cache occasionally if it starts running slowly, and make sure your phone's operating system is reasonably up to date. If the app crashes or the quiz does not load, first check your internet connection, then close the app fully and reopen it. If the problem continues, uninstall and reinstall the app from the official store. As a last step, contact Ufone support through the app's help section and describe what happens when the problem occurs.

Please note: quiz answers, codes, prices, packages and rewards change regularly. Always confirm the current details in the official My Ufone app before you subscribe, pay or rely on any code listed here.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the My Ufone app free?
Yes, the app is free to download and use. Normal mobile data charges may apply when you use it on mobile data, depending on your package.

Can I use the app on Wi-Fi?
Yes. The app works on Wi-Fi as well as mobile data, and using Wi-Fi avoids using your mobile data allowance.

What if the one-time code does not arrive?
Check your signal, wait a few minutes, then request a new code. Never share the code with anyone who asks for it.
