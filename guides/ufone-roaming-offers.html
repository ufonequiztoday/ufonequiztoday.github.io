Ufone Roaming Offers for Travellers

By Ufone Quiz Today · Updated for 2026 · Helpful guide for Ufone users in Pakistan

International roaming lets you use your Ufone SIM in another country, so you can receive calls and messages and use data while travelling. Roaming can be expensive if you use it without a plan, so it pays to understand the offers before you travel. This guide explains how roaming works, what offers are available, and how to keep costs under control. Confirm current roaming rates and offers in the official My Ufone app or on Ufone's website before you travel, because they change by country.

What roaming is and how it works

When you travel abroad, your phone connects to a local network in the country you are visiting, through an agreement with Ufone. Your Ufone number keeps working, so people can still call and message you, and you can use data, but the charges are set by the roaming agreement and are usually higher than at home. Your phone will show the name of the local network it has connected to.

Roaming is normally switched on automatically when you arrive in another country, but it is wise to check before you travel. Open your phone's settings and make sure data roaming is set the way you want it, because an app updating in the background on roaming data can use an expensive amount very quickly.

Roaming offers and bundles

Ufone offers roaming bundles for popular destinations, which give you a set amount of data, minutes or SMS for a fixed price and a fixed number of days. These bundles are much cheaper than paying standard roaming rates, so buy one before you travel if a bundle covers your destination. The app shows the bundles available for the country you are visiting, along with their price and validity.

Bundles are specific to regions or countries, so a bundle for one country may not work in another. Check that your destination is covered before you buy, and note the validity period, because a bundle that lasts seven days will not cover a two-week trip. Buy a second bundle if your trip is longer than the first bundle's validity.

Costs to watch out for

The biggest roaming cost is usually data. Streaming video, backing up photos, and automatic app updates can use large amounts of data at roaming rates. Turn off automatic updates and photo backup on mobile data before you travel, and use hotel or cafe Wi-Fi for heavy use. Receiving calls while roaming can also be charged, so check the rates for incoming calls in your destination.

SMS is usually the cheapest way to stay in touch while roaming, and receiving SMS is often free. Messaging apps used over Wi-Fi cost nothing, so they are the best choice for long conversations. Keep an eye on your balance in the app while you travel, so a large charge does not surprise you on your return.

Before you travel: a checklist

A week before you travel, check the roaming offers for your destination in the app and buy a suitable bundle. Make sure your balance is enough to cover the bundle and any extra use. Check that your phone is unlocked for international use and that data roaming is switched on. Download offline maps and any entertainment you want before you leave, so you do not need to stream them abroad.

Tell your family how to reach you, and note the helpline number in case you need support while away. Save important numbers offline, because you may not have data when you first arrive. A little preparation before departure saves both money and stress during the trip.

When you return home

When you return to Pakistan, your phone will reconnect to the Ufone network automatically. Check your balance in the app to see what you spent, and check whether any roaming bundle is still active, so you can cancel it if it is no longer needed. If a charge looks wrong, note the date and amount and contact support with the details, because roaming charges can sometimes be disputed if they are incorrect.

Keep your travel receipts and the bundle confirmation messages until you have checked the final bill. If everything matches, you can delete them. If anything is unclear, support can explain the charges with your records in front of them.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need a roaming bundle to use my Ufone SIM abroad?
Your SIM may work abroad without a bundle, but standard roaming rates are high. A roaming bundle for your destination is much cheaper, so buy one before you travel.

Is receiving SMS free while roaming?
Receiving SMS is often free while roaming, but check the rates for your destination in the app, as they vary by country.

How do I avoid a big roaming bill?
Buy a roaming bundle before you travel, use Wi-Fi for heavy data, turn off automatic updates, and check your balance in the app while you travel.
