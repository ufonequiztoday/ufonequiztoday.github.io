Ufone SMS Bundles and Codes

By Ufone Quiz Today · Updated for 2026 · Helpful guide for Ufone users in Pakistan

Text messaging is less central than it used to be, because most people now use WhatsApp and other internet messaging apps. Even so, SMS is still needed for bank alerts, verification codes, and messages to people who do not use the internet. Ufone offers SMS bundles for users who send many texts, and this guide explains how they work, when they are worth buying, and how to subscribe. Confirm current prices in the official My Ufone app.

What an SMS bundle gives you

An SMS bundle gives you a set number of text messages for a fixed price and a fixed validity, often a day, a week or a month. Once the bundle is active, each text you send uses one SMS from the bundle instead of charging your main balance. This is cheaper than paying for each message separately if you send many texts. Most bundles cover SMS to all networks in Pakistan, but check the terms in the app, because some bundles only cover on-net messages.

Bundles are sold in different sizes. A small bundle suits someone who sends a few texts a day, while a large bundle suits someone who sends dozens. Because most people now send most messages over the internet, many users need only a small SMS bundle, or none at all, and the SMS included in a Super Card is often enough.

Do you still need an SMS bundle

Ask yourself how many SMS you actually sent last month. Your phone's messaging app can show this, or you can estimate from your usage. If the number is small, paying per SMS from your balance may be cheaper than a bundle. If you send many SMS every day, for work or to family members without smartphones, a bundle will save you money.

Remember that verification codes and bank messages you receive do not use your SMS bundle, because receiving SMS is normally free. Only messages you send count against the bundle. Also, messages sent through WhatsApp, Messenger or other internet apps do not use SMS at all, so they never touch your bundle.

SMS bundles inside a Super Card

Many Ufone users get their SMS through a Super Card rather than a separate bundle, because the Super Card includes a set of SMS alongside data and minutes. If you already have a Super Card, check how many SMS it includes before buying a separate bundle, because you may already have enough. Buying a separate bundle on top of a Super Card usually only makes sense if you send far more texts than the Super Card covers.

The app shows your remaining SMS clearly, so check it before you buy. If you regularly run out of SMS before the month ends, either your Super Card is too small or you need a separate bundle. If SMS are left over every month, you are paying for messages you never send.

How to subscribe to an SMS bundle

Open the My Ufone app, go to the bundles section, and look for SMS bundles. Choose the size and validity that fits your usage, check the price, and confirm. Make sure you have enough balance first. You will receive a confirmation message, and the app will show your remaining SMS and the expiry date. You can also subscribe using a USSD code shown in the app, but the app is clearer because it shows the details before you pay.

After subscribing, send a test message to a family member to confirm the bundle is active. If the message is charged to your balance instead of the bundle, check the app to confirm the bundle is active and contact support if the problem continues.

What to do when SMS do not send

If your messages fail to send, first check that you have signal and that the recipient's number is correct. Then check your remaining SMS and balance in the app, because an expired bundle or empty balance will block sending. Restart your phone if the problem continues, and check that your message centre number is set correctly in your phone's messaging settings. If messages still fail, contact Ufone support, because there may be a network issue or a problem with your SIM.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many SMS do I get in a bundle?
It depends on the bundle size. The app shows the exact number of SMS, price and validity for each bundle before you subscribe.

Are incoming SMS free?
Yes, receiving SMS is normally free. Only messages you send use your bundle or balance.

Can I send SMS to other networks with a bundle?
Most SMS bundles cover all networks in Pakistan, but check the terms in the app, as some bundles are on-net only.
