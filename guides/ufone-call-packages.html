Ufone Call Packages Guide

By Ufone Quiz Today · Updated for 2026 · Helpful guide for Ufone users in Pakistan

If you make many phone calls, a call package can save you a lot of money compared with paying standard per-minute rates. Ufone offers call bundles for calls to Ufone numbers, calls to other networks, and combined bundles that include both. This guide explains the main types of call packages, how minutes work, and how to choose the one that fits your calling habits. Confirm current prices and inclusions in the official My Ufone app, as they change regularly.

On-net call bundles

On-net minutes are for calls to Ufone numbers and usually to PTCL numbers as well. These bundles are the cheapest per minute, because calls within the same network cost the operator less. If most of your friends and family use Ufone, an on-net bundle covers almost all of your calls at a low price. Some bundles offer a large number of on-net minutes for a whole month, which suits people who talk for a long time every day.

Check who you actually call before choosing. Look at your recent calls and count how many were to Ufone numbers. If the answer is most of them, an on-net bundle is the right starting point, and you can add a small off-net bundle for the rest.

Off-net call bundles

Off-net minutes are for calls to other networks, such as Jazz, Telenor and Zong. These bundles cost more per minute than on-net bundles, because the operator pays the other network for carrying the call. If your contacts are spread across several networks, you need off-net minutes, or a combined bundle that includes both on-net and off-net minutes.

A common mistake is buying a large on-net bundle and then discovering that most of your calls are off-net. The minutes sit unused while your balance drains on off-net calls. A combined bundle, such as those included in a Super Card, avoids this problem by giving you both types of minutes in one package.

Hourly and daily call offers

Some Ufone call offers cover a short period, such as a few hours in the evening or a single day. These suit people who make a long call at a particular time, for example a daily evening call to family. They are cheap because they only cover a limited window, so they are a poor choice if you call at all hours of the day. Read the time window carefully before subscribing, because calls outside the window are charged at normal rates.

Hourly offers are also useful as a one-off, for example when you need to make a long call today and do not want a monthly bundle. For regular daily calling, a weekly or monthly bundle is usually cheaper overall.

Monthly call bundles

Monthly call bundles give you a set number of minutes for 30 days. They suit people who call every day and want predictable costs. A monthly bundle removes the worry of watching the clock during calls, because you know how many minutes you have left and when they expire. Larger bundles suit people who talk for hours each week, while smaller ones suit people who make a few short calls a day.

As with data, check your real usage before choosing. Your phone's call history shows how many minutes you used last month, and the app shows your remaining minutes. Choose a bundle slightly larger than your normal use, so you are not caught short in the last week of the month.

How to subscribe and check minutes

Subscribe through the My Ufone app by opening the call bundles section, choosing the bundle that fits, and confirming. The app shows the price, the number of minutes and the validity before you pay. After subscribing, check your remaining minutes in the app whenever you like. The app shows how many minutes are left and when they expire, so you can plan your calls.

If your minutes run out, calls are charged from your main balance at standard rates, which is more expensive. If that happens often, your bundle is too small and you should move to a larger one next month. If minutes are left over every month, move to a smaller bundle and save the difference.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between on-net and off-net minutes?
On-net minutes are for calls to Ufone and PTCL numbers. Off-net minutes are for calls to other networks, and they usually cost more.

Do unused call minutes carry over?
Normally no. Unused minutes usually expire at the end of the bundle's validity, so check the expiry date in the app.

How do I check my remaining call minutes?
Open the My Ufone app. Your remaining minutes, data and SMS are shown on the home screen with their expiry dates.
