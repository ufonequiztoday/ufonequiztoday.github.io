Ufone Balance Check Code

By Ufone Quiz Today · Updated for 2026 · Helpful guide for Ufone users in Pakistan

Knowing your remaining balance helps you avoid running out of credit in the middle of a call or while using data. Ufone offers more than one way to check it: a short USSD code dialled from your phone, and the My Ufone app. This guide explains both methods, what the reply means, and what to do if a balance check does not work. As with all codes, confirm the current code in the official app, because codes can change.

The balance check code

The widely published Ufone balance check code is *124#. To use it, open your phone's dialler, type *124# and press the call button. Within a few seconds you should receive a message on your screen showing your remaining balance. The reply usually shows your main balance, and sometimes it also shows the expiry date of your balance. Write down the result if you need it, because the on-screen message disappears when you dismiss it.

Some users are charged a small fee for a balance check, depending on their package and the current terms, so check the app or helpline if you want to know the current charge. If you check your balance very often, the app method below is usually the better choice, because it shows more detail and does not use a code at all.

Checking balance in the My Ufone app

Open the My Ufone app and sign in with your Ufone number. Your remaining balance is shown on the home screen, usually at the top. Tapping it opens a detailed view with your remaining data, call minutes and SMS, along with the expiry date of each. This is the most complete way to check your balance, because it shows everything in one place and updates whenever your usage changes.

The app also keeps a history of your recent usage, so you can see what used your balance. If a charge looks wrong, note the date and amount shown in the app before contacting support, because that detail helps them investigate. The app is free to use, and checking your balance in the app does not cost extra.

Loading balance with a scratch card

If you buy a scratch card, gently scratch off the silver panel to reveal the card number, then dial *123* followed by the card number and #, and press call. For example, if the card number is 12345678901234, you would dial *123*12345678901234#. You should receive a confirmation message showing your new balance. Keep the card until the balance is confirmed, in case the load fails and you need the number for support.

You can also load balance through the app, using a bank card or mobile wallet, if that option is available in your area. Always confirm the current load methods and any charges in the official app, because they can change. Never share your scratch card number with anyone before you load it, because anyone with the number can use it.

What the balance message means

Your main balance is the money you can use for calls, SMS and data at standard rates. Some balances are split into main balance and bonus balance, and bonus balance may only work for certain services or may expire sooner. The expiry date matters: if your balance expires, you may lose the remaining amount, so use it or recharge before that date. The app shows these details more clearly than the code reply, so check there if anything is unclear.

If your balance drops faster than expected, check your active packages and data usage in the app. Background apps can use mobile data without you noticing, and a package that expired may have switched you to standard rates. Turning off mobile data for apps you do not use, and using Wi-Fi where possible, helps your balance last longer.

When a balance check does not work

If *124# gives no reply, first check that you have signal and that you typed the code correctly, including the * at the start and the # at the end. Try again after a minute. If it still fails, restart your phone and try once more. If the code keeps failing, use the app instead, and contact Ufone support if both methods fail, because there may be a temporary network issue in your area.

Also check that your SIM is active and that you are using a Ufone SIM. The code only works on the Ufone network, so it will not work from another operator's SIM. If you recently changed phones, make sure the SIM is inserted correctly and the phone is not in aeroplane mode.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the Ufone balance check code?
The widely published code is *124#. Dial it from your Ufone number and your remaining balance appears on screen. Confirm the current code in the My Ufone app, as codes can change.

Can I check my balance for free?
The app shows your balance without a code charge. Code-based checks may carry a small fee depending on current terms, so check the app for the latest information.

Why did my balance check show nothing?
It is usually a weak signal or a typing mistake. Check your signal, retype the code carefully, and try the My Ufone app as an alternative.
