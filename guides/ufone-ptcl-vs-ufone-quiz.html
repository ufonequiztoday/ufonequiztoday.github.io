Ufone Quiz vs PTCL and Other Quizzes

By Ufone Quiz Today · Updated for 2026 · Helpful guide for Ufone users in Pakistan

Ufone is not the only company in Pakistan offering quizzes and rewards. Other mobile networks run their own quiz and rewards programmes, and PTCL, as Ufone's parent company, offers its own services and promotions. This guide compares the Ufone daily quiz with the alternatives fairly, so you can decide where to spend your few minutes a day. The comparison is based on how these programmes generally work, and current details should always be confirmed in each company's official app.

The Ufone quiz at a glance

The Ufone daily quiz lives inside the My Ufone app, offers a short daily round of general-knowledge questions, and rewards players with small bonuses such as free data. Its strengths are simplicity and regularity: one round a day, a few minutes to play, and a reward that is credited to the same number. It suits Ufone customers who already use the app to check their balance and packages, because the quiz is right there on the home screen.

Its limits are the same as any small daily game. The rewards are modest, the questions change daily, and you need a Ufone SIM to take part. It will not replace a data package, and it is not a way to earn money. Players who enjoy it do so because it is quick, free and a little rewarding, not because the prizes are large.

PTCL and the Ufone connection

PTCL is Ufone's parent company, and the two share ownership, but they serve different needs. PTCL provides landline, broadband and television services to homes and offices, while Ufone provides mobile services. PTCL runs its own promotions and rewards for its broadband and landline customers, but these are not the same as the Ufone mobile quiz, and a PTCL connection does not give you access to the Ufone quiz.

If your household uses both PTCL broadband and Ufone mobile, you can enjoy both companies' offers separately. The Ufone quiz remains a mobile-only game tied to a Ufone number, while PTCL promotions are tied to a PTCL account. There is no single combined quiz, so compare them as two separate programmes.

Other mobile network quizzes

Jazz, Telenor and Zong each run their own rewards and games inside their apps, and they work in a broadly similar way: open the app, play a game or complete a task, and earn a small bonus. The differences are in the details: the size of the reward, the type of game, how often you can play, and how easy the app is to use. Some networks emphasise daily check-ins, others emphasise quizzes, and some offer spin-the-wheel games.

The honest way to compare them is to try the one that matches the SIM you already use. Switching networks just for a quiz game rarely makes sense, because the rewards are small and every network's game is similar in value. The best quiz for you is almost always the one on the network you already use every day.

Which quiz gives the best rewards

Reward sizes change with every campaign, so any ranking made today may be wrong next month. A network that offers a generous reward this month may offer a smaller one next month, and the reverse is also true. Instead of chasing the biggest advertised reward, look at the whole picture: how often you can play, how easy the game is, and whether the reward is useful to you, such as data you will actually use.

A small data reward on the network you use daily is worth more than a larger reward on a network whose SIM sits unused in a drawer. Regular play on your main number also builds your knowledge and your habit, which improves your score over time. That steady value matters more than a one-off prize.

Playing quizzes on more than one network

Some people carry two SIMs and play the quiz on each network's app. This is allowed, because each quiz belongs to its own network and its own number. It takes more time, because you play two rounds a day instead of one, but it doubles your chances of a reward. Keep the accounts separate, play each quiz from the number it belongs to, and never share codes or passwords between accounts.

If you do play on two networks, keep your expectations realistic. Two small rewards are still small, and the time spent playing two quizzes every day adds up. Play the second quiz only if you genuinely enjoy it, not because you expect a large payout.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the Ufone quiz better than other networks' quizzes?
They are broadly similar: a short daily game with a small reward. The best one for you is usually the quiz on the network you already use every day.

Can I play the Ufone quiz with a PTCL connection?
No. The Ufone quiz needs a Ufone mobile number and the My Ufone app. PTCL broadband and landline accounts have their own separate promotions.

Can I play quizzes on two different networks?
Yes, if you have a SIM on each network. Each quiz is tied to its own number, so play each one from the matching account.
