Ufone Quiz Categories and How to Prepare

By Ufone Quiz Today · Updated for 2026 · Helpful guide for Ufone users in Pakistan

Daily quiz questions come from several broad categories, and knowing what those categories are helps you prepare in a focused way. Instead of studying everything at once, you can spend a few minutes on each category and build a solid base across all of them. This guide explains the main categories you are likely to meet, with examples of the facts each one tests.

Pakistan general knowledge

Pakistan GK is the most important category for the Ufone quiz, because the game is made for Pakistani players. Questions often cover the capital city, the national animal, bird, flower, fruit and game, the national language, the currency, the largest city, the longest river, and the highest mountain. Learn these national symbols first, because they are the easiest marks in the quiz.

Other common Pakistan questions cover the provinces and their capitals, famous historical figures, the national poet, the date of independence, and major dams and rivers. Reading a short Pakistan studies summary once a week keeps these facts fresh. If you learn only one category well, make it this one.

World geography

Geography questions test capitals, rivers, mountains, deserts, oceans and famous landmarks. Learn the capitals of major countries first, then the largest and longest features: the largest desert, the longest river, the highest mountain, and the largest ocean. Maps help more than lists, because seeing where places are makes them easier to remember.

A good method is to learn one region at a time. Spend a day on Europe, then a day on Asia, then Africa and the Americas. After a month you will know most of the capitals and major features that quizzes ask about. Our practice tool includes geography questions to get you started.

Science and technology

Science questions usually cover basic facts rather than advanced theory: the chemical symbol for common elements, the planet closest to the sun, the gas plants absorb, the speed of light, the inventor of common devices, and the human body's basic facts. These are the kind of facts found in school science books, so revising a school-level science summary is excellent preparation.

Technology questions cover the founders of famous companies, the meaning of common abbreviations, and the history of the internet and mobile phones. Keep up with technology news, and learn the full forms of abbreviations like SIM, SMS, GPS and USB, because they appear in quizzes regularly.

Sports

Sports questions focus on the most popular games: cricket, hockey, football and squash. Learn the national game of Pakistan, the number of players in a cricket team, the length of a cricket pitch, famous Pakistani sports stars, and the winners of recent major tournaments. Sports questions are often about recent events, so following sports news helps a great deal.

Also learn the basic facts of the Olympic Games, the FIFA World Cup and the Cricket World Cup, such as how often they are held and where the latest one took place. These large events produce quiz questions for years after they happen.

History and current affairs

History questions cover famous rulers, wars, independence movements, ancient civilisations and important dates. Learn the key dates in Pakistan's history and the major events of world history, such as the world wars and the space race. Current-affairs questions cover recent news, new leaders, elections and major events, so reading the news daily is the best preparation for this category.

A simple habit covers both: read the headlines each morning and note one new fact a day. Over a month you will have thirty new facts, and over a year you will have hundreds. That steady habit is what separates regular high scorers from occasional players.

Frequently Asked Questions

What categories does the Ufone quiz cover?
The main categories are Pakistan GK, world geography, science, sports, history and current affairs. The exact mix changes daily.

Which category should I study first?
Pakistan GK, because it appears most often and the facts are the easiest to learn. National symbols and capitals are a good starting point.

How do I prepare for current-affairs questions?
Read the news headlines for a few minutes each day. Current-affairs questions usually come from the biggest stories of the past few weeks.
