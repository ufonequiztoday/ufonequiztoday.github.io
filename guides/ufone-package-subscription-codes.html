Ufone Package Subscription Codes (Complete List)

By Ufone Quiz Today · Updated for 2026 · Helpful guide for Ufone users in Pakistan

USSD codes are short numbers you dial from your phone to check your balance, subscribe to a package, or reach a service. They work without the internet, which makes them useful when your data has run out. This page lists the well-known Ufone codes in one place, explains what each one does, and warns you where codes can change. Always confirm the current code in the official My Ufone app before you rely on it.

Balance and load codes

The most used code is *124#, which shows your remaining balance on screen after you dial it. It is the quickest way to check your balance when you have no internet access. To load balance from a scratch card, dial *123*, then the card number, then #. For example, a card numbered 12345678901234 is loaded by dialling *123*12345678901234#. Keep the card until the load is confirmed.

These two codes are the ones most Ufone users need every day. Learn them by heart, and use the app when you want more detail, such as your remaining data and the expiry dates of your packages.

Package and Super Card codes

Ufone package subscriptions are commonly handled through the code *770#, which opens a menu of packages including the Super Card. Dial *770#, follow the on-screen menu, and choose the package you want. The menu shows the options available on your number, so you can pick the right one without remembering a separate code for each package.

Some packages also have their own direct codes, but these change more often than the main menu code, so the menu is the safer route. If a direct code you found online does not work, use the *770# menu or the app instead. Never trust a code from an unknown source that asks you to send money or share a number.

Checking remaining bundles

The code *706# is commonly listed for checking your remaining bundle resources, such as data, minutes and SMS left in your active packages. Dial it and follow the reply on your screen. If it does not work on your number, the My Ufone app shows the same information in more detail, with expiry dates included, so the app is a good fallback.

Checking your remaining resources regularly helps you avoid surprises. A quick check once or twice a week shows whether your bundle will last until the end of the month, or whether you need a top-up.

Helpline and other useful numbers

The Ufone helpline is 333, which you call like a normal phone number from your Ufone SIM. It connects you to customer support for balance problems, package questions, network complaints and general help. Keep this number in your contacts so you can reach support quickly when you need it.

Other useful short numbers may appear in the app or on Ufone's official website, for example numbers for specific services or complaints. Only use numbers published by Ufone itself, and be careful of numbers shared in random messages or videos, because they may connect you to scammers.

How to use USSD codes safely

Type codes carefully, including the * at the start and the # at the end, because a single wrong digit can subscribe you to the wrong package or fail completely. Read the reply on your screen fully before pressing anything else, and do not rush through menus. If a menu asks you to confirm a payment, check the amount and the package name before you confirm.

Keep a note of the codes you use most often, and check the app once in a while to see whether any code has changed. Codes are updated from time to time, and an old code found in an old article may no longer work. The app is always the most up-to-date source.

Please note: quiz answers, codes, prices, packages and rewards change regularly. Always confirm the current details in the official My Ufone app before you subscribe, pay or rely on any code listed here.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the Ufone balance check code?
The widely published code is *124#. Dial it from your Ufone number to see your remaining balance on screen.

How do I subscribe to a Ufone package by code?
Dial *770# to open the package menu, then choose the package you want. Confirm the current code and price in the My Ufone app first.

Why does a code sometimes not work?
Codes change over time, and some codes only work on certain packages or numbers. Check the current code in the app and try the app itself as an alternative.
