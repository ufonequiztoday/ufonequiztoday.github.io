Ufone Quiz Not Working? Troubleshooting Guide

By Ufone Quiz Today · Updated for 2026 · Helpful guide for Ufone users in Pakistan

It is frustrating when the daily quiz will not load, freezes halfway through, or shows an error instead of questions. Most quiz problems have simple causes, such as a weak connection, an outdated app, or a temporary server issue, and most can be fixed in a few minutes. This guide walks through the fixes in order, from the quickest to the most thorough, so you can get back to playing.

Check your internet connection first

The quiz needs a stable internet connection, so start there. Check whether other apps load normally. If nothing loads, your connection is the problem, not the quiz. Switch between mobile data and Wi-Fi to see which one works better, and move to a place with a stronger signal if you are indoors. Turn aeroplane mode on and off once to reset the connection.

If you are on mobile data, check that you have data left in your balance, because the quiz cannot load without it. Open the app's home screen to see your remaining data, or dial *124# to check your balance. If your data has run out, connect to Wi-Fi or buy a small data bundle before trying the quiz again.

Update and restart the app

An outdated app is a common cause of quiz problems, because Ufone updates the quiz layout and features regularly. Open the Play Store or App Store, search for the My Ufone app, and install any available update. Then close the app fully, remove it from your recent apps, and open it again. This simple restart fixes many loading problems.

If the quiz still does not load after an update, clear the app's cache in your phone's settings. This removes temporary files that may be causing the problem, without deleting your account or settings. Do not clear the app's data unless support tells you to, because that signs you out and removes saved settings.

Sign out and sign back in

If the quiz section is missing or shows an error, signing out and back in can refresh your account. Sign out from the app's settings or profile section, then sign in again with your Ufone number and the one-time code sent by SMS. This refreshes your account details and often restores missing features such as the quiz card.

Never share the one-time code with anyone during this process. The code is sent to your phone and should only be entered in the app itself. If the code does not arrive, check your signal and request a new one after a few minutes.

Reinstall the app

If updating, restarting and signing in again do not fix the problem, uninstall the My Ufone app and install it fresh from the official store. A fresh install removes corrupted files that an update cannot fix. After reinstalling, sign in with your number and check whether the quiz appears on the home screen.

Make sure you install the official app by checking the developer name before downloading. Copycat apps with similar names exist, and they will not have the real quiz. A fresh install from the official store is safe and takes only a few minutes on a good connection.

When the problem is on Ufone's side

Sometimes the quiz is unavailable because Ufone's servers are busy or the quiz campaign is paused between rounds. In that case, no fix on your phone will help, and the best step is to wait and try again later in the day or the next morning. Check whether other app features work normally; if they do, the problem is likely limited to the quiz itself.

If the problem lasts more than a day, contact Ufone support through the app's help section or the helpline at 333. Describe what happens when you try to open the quiz, including any error message shown, and mention the steps you already tried. That helps support find the cause without asking you to repeat the same fixes.

Please note: quiz answers, codes, prices, packages and rewards change regularly. Always confirm the current details in the official My Ufone app before you subscribe, pay or rely on any code listed here.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why is the Ufone quiz not loading?
The most common causes are a weak connection, an outdated app, or a temporary server issue. Check your connection, update the app, and try again.

The quiz card is missing from my app. What do I do?
Update the app, then sign out and sign back in. If the card is still missing, the quiz may be paused between campaigns, so check again the next day.

The quiz froze halfway through. Will I lose my reward?
If the round did not finish, the system may not have recorded it. Try again the next day, and contact support with a screenshot if a completed round's reward is missing.
